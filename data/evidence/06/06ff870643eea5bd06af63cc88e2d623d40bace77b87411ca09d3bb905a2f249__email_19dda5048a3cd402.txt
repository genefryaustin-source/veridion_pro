Meet DataStore: your Applets just got a memory
DataStore ( https://ifttt.com/explore/introducing-datastore-on-ifttt ) gives your Applets a shared memory, a place to store information that persists between runs and is available across your whole setup.
A few things DataStore makes possible:
⚡️ No more 3AM notification floods
⚡️ Track timestamps and counters across automations
⚡️ Coordinate multiple Applets like one intelligent system
